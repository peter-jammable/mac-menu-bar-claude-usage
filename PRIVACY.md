# Privacy Policy

ClawUsage does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## What we access

- **Claude OAuth tokens**: Stored locally in your macOS Keychain to authenticate with the Claude API. Never transmitted anywhere except to Anthropic's servers.
- **Rate limit data**: Fetched from Anthropic's API and displayed locally. Not stored or transmitted elsewhere.

## What we don't do

- No analytics or tracking
- No data collection
- No third-party services
- No network requests except to Anthropic's API

## Data storage

All data stays on your device in the macOS Keychain. Signing out deletes all stored tokens.
